# BUA Cement PLC vs HMBNG(Lafarge) Financial Performance Analysis

## Project Overview.
This project presents a five-year comparative financial analysis of **BUA Cement Plc and HBMNG (formerly Lafarge Africa Plc)**, covering the financial years 2021–2025.

The analysis aims to support a high-net-worth client's proposed ₦25 billion investment in Nigeria's industrial goods sector, with a focus on cement production, driven by the country's urbanisation and infrastructure development needs.

Using published annual reports and financial statements, the project evaluates both companies' profitability, liquidity, solvency, operational efficiency, cash flow performance, and growth trends.

The objective is to identify each company's financial strengths and weaknesses, assess their relative investment attractiveness, and provide an evidence-based recommendation to guide the client's investment decision.

- Can the business withstand financial pressure?

## Key Findings 
- **Revenue Growth:** BUA cement recorded revenue growth of 43.1%, compared with 39.1% for HBM, difference of 4%. This suggests that BUA is expanding its sales at a faster rate. If sustained, stronger revenue growth could help BUA increase earnings and cash generation over time. 
- **Profitability:** BUA Cement recorded a higher average net profit margin of 23.3%, compared with 18.2% for HBM (Lafarge) Cement. Reflecting better cost management, pricing power, or a more profitable product strategy.
- **Shareholder Returns:** BUA recorded a higher average Return on Equity (ROE) of 30.4%, compared with 20.3% for HBM (Lafarge), suggesting a strong returns on shareholder’s capital.
- **Liquidity:** HBM has stronger short-term liquidity position and may be better placed to handle immediate cash obligations. 
- **Free Cash Flow:** BUA generates about 20.5% above HBM in absolute free cash flow, which provides greater financial flexibility to support debt repayment, dividends, share buybacks, acquisitions, and reinvestment in the business.
- **Financial Risk:** HBM (Lafarge) maintained a significantly lower debt-to-equity ratio, suggesting a more conservative debt financing and may face lower interest and refinancing risks.
- **Valuation:** HBM (Lafarge) recorded a lower average P/E ratio, making it cheaper relative to its reported earnings during the period.

## Conclusion
Based on the comparative analysis of FY2021 - FY2025 financial performance, BUA cement PLC is the preferred company over HBMNG (Lafarge) cement. The company recorded stronger revenue growth, profitability, higher returns on equity, and greater free cash flow generation, indicating better operating performance and growth potential. However, its higher debt-to-equity and P/E ratios suggest greater financial risk and a more expensive valuation.
